Cost proxy = P&I + existing mortgaged owners’ tax and insurance proxies + PMI scenario. Separate county medians do not sum to an observed median bill. PMI: $30–$70/month per $100,000 borrowed when down payment is below 20%. Closing costs: 2–5% of value. HOA, maintenance, utilities, reserves, assets and other debt are excluded. Income-MOE classifications hold home values fixed and do not include cost uncertainty.
